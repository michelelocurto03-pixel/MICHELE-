Data Warehouse Modeling Choices

1.1 Business Process & Fact Grain

The chosen business process is the monitoring and execution of financial transactions (BUY/SELL) against a personal investment account during the 2024 fiscal year.

Fact grain: one row in Fact_Transactions = one single financial transaction recorded in the source account-statement file. Keeping the atomic grain allows every required aggregation — by quarter, country, sector, or industry — to be answered without pre-aggregation or information loss.

1.2 Dimension Design

The schema adopts a classic Star Schema with one central fact table and four surrounding dimension tables:

TableTypeKey AttributesDesign Rationale
Fact_TransactionsFactSK_Time, SK_Geography*, SK_Symbol*, SK_Type, QuantityHolds the additive measure (units traded) and all foreign keys
Dim_TimeDimensionDate, Day, Month, Quarter, Year, DayOfWeekEnables full temporal drill-down from day to year
Dim_GeographyDimensionCountry, ISO_Code, Region, Sub_RegionGeopolitical segmentation based on issuing company headquarters
Dim_SymbolDimensionSymbol, Company, Sector, IndustryCorporate classification hierarchy from ticker to macro sector
Dim_Transaction_TypeDimensionTransaction_Type (BUY / SELL / DIVIDENT)Flat dimension distinguishing the nature of each financial operation

* Nullable foreign keys: SK_Geography and SK_Symbol were intentionally designed as optional, to avoid inflating sector/country counts with dummy "Unknown" records for the 18 orphan European tickers.

1.3 Dimension Hierarchies

Dim_Time
Day → Month → Quarter → Year

+ DayOfWeek as a standalone attribute

Dim_Geography
Country → Sub-Region → Region
Dim_Symbol
Symbol → Company → Industry → Sector
Dim_Transaction_Type

Flat dimension — no hierarchy. Values: BUY, SELL, DIVIDENT.

1.4 Degenerate Dimension

The field idtransaction is retained directly in Fact_Transactions as a degenerate dimension. It carries no dedicated table but provides a direct audit trail back to the operational source — standard practice in financial data warehouses.

Data Quality Issues Found

Dataset summary after loading: 2,281 total rows — BUY: 1,082  |  SELL: 1,089  |  DIVIDENT: 110  |  Unique symbols: 129  |  Countries: 22  |  Sectors: 10  |  Industries: 57
2.1 Country Name Mismatches Fixed

Two country names in symbols.csv did not match their official ISO form in country.csv, which would have silently produced NULL matches on the geographic join.

Value in symbols.csvCorrect ISO Name
TaiwanTaiwan, Province of China
TurkeyTürkiye

Additionally, the Taiwan record was missing region/sub_region values — patched at extraction time.

2.2 Orphan Symbols 18 tickers

18 ticker symbols present in the transaction log had no corresponding record in symbols.csv. These are primarily Italian and European exchange instruments (e.g. ENI.MI, ENEL.MI) not covered by the static master file — approximately 2% of total transactions.

Decision: retain with SK_Symbol = NULL. These rows are excluded from sector/industry analytics but included in all volume and temporal aggregations, preserving mathematical correctness.
2.3 Unexpected Transaction Type DIVIDENT

The source dataset contained a third transaction type DIVIDENT (likely a misspelling of DIVIDEND), not anticipated by the homework specification. Rather than discarding these 110 records, Dim_Transaction_Type was extended to accommodate all three values.

Dividend entries are excluded from trade-count analytics but preserved in the fact table for completeness.

2.4 Missing Values Resolved

After removing rows with missing mandatory fields (date, symbol, transactiontype) and invalid transaction types, zero missing values remained in the core analytical columns.

The cleaning pipeline reduced the raw row count by less than 0.5%.
